Tiny Big Facts

Big science, explained small: short animated science videos on YouTube.

Tiny Big Facts is a YouTube channel of hand-drawn-style stick-figure animations that explain surprising science: why the Moon doesn't fall down, why lightning is hotter than the Sun's surface, and many more everyday mysteries.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@TinyBigFacts

Tiny Big Facts Uploader

"Tiny Big Facts Uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uploads the channel's own original videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API, and schedules when they are published. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access any other person's account or data.
