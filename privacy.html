Privacy Policy

Tiny Big Facts Uploader · Last updated 7 November 2026

Tiny Big Facts Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Tiny Big Facts YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, and to read the channel's name so the owner can confirm the right channel is connected.
  • It does not read, collect or store anyone else's data, comments, viewing history, contacts or email.

How data is stored

  • The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the app's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Tiny Big Facts channel page.
